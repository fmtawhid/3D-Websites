01 / THE ZR1

A new apex for Corvette.

The C8 ZR1 pairs a 5.5-liter, flat-plane-crank V8 with twin turbochargers to deliver 1,064 hp and 828 lb-ft of torque.

Its mid-engine layout, advanced cooling, available carbon-fiber aero, and an 8-speed dual-clutch transmission are engineered to make immense power usable on road and track.
